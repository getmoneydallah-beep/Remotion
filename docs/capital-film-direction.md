# «ليش أول 100 ألف أصعب من أول مليون؟» — Creative Direction

## Phase 1 — Audit of the current reel (FinanceReel v1)

| Problem | Why it reads as amateur |
|---|---|
| Gold-gradient text with glow and drop shadows on black | It's the visual language of crypto ads and motivational reels. The gold carries no meaning, so it reads as a "premium" costume. |
| Every scene uses the same layout: headline on top, widget in the middle, disclaimer at the bottom | It's a slide template repeated five times. There's no change of shot scale, so the eye never gets a new space. |
| Every element enters the same way: spring, scale, blur and fade | This is effectively one transition used everywhere. With nothing to contrast against, nothing feels important. |
| All type is weight 700–900 | There's no hierarchy. When everything shouts, nothing is emphasised, and Arabic set heavy at large sizes looks crude. |
| Floating particles, rotating light rays and a pulsing ring | These are decorative effects with no story job. They signal "cheap template". |
| Progress bar, counter, line chart and comparison cards | It's dashboard UI, which explains the idea but never lets the viewer *feel* it. |
| Number counters carry the story | The numbers are the subject instead of evidence. There's no physical metaphor to hold them. |
| The music is a loop of chords with whooshes on every cut | It's a generic corporate bed. There's no silence, so nothing hits. |

## Phase 2 — Three art directions

### A. «الكتلة» — Mass & Gravity (2.5D physical metaphor)
Capital is a physical body. At first you carry every grain to it yourself, one at a time, in a slow, monotonous rhythm. Past a certain mass it starts to exert its own gravity: grains begin spiralling in without you. A single off-white dot, the first riyal, is the protagonist from the first frame to the last, and it finally lands as the full stop of «ابدأ اليوم.».
- **Colour logic:** off-white is money made by effort, and cobalt is money made by capital. The meaning carries the colour, not the other way around.
- **Motion philosophy:** physics. Hand-placed deposits arrive at a constant, tiring tempo, then accelerating orbits take over. The camera works like a lens: an extreme close-up on the grains, a wide pull-back to reveal the orbits, a diagram for the comparison, then a pull-back to a single point.
- **Typography:** an editorial magazine system (IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Light for display, Medium only for emphasis) with Plex Mono for numerals and chapter markers, hairline rules and masked line reveals.

### B. «الدفتر» — The Ledger (Swiss editorial, flat)
An FT / Stripe Press look: a warm off-white page, charcoal ink, one cobalt rule. The story is told with typographic numbers and precise line drawings on a strict grid. Transitions are page turns and grid re-flows.
- **Strengths:** very restrained and very readable.
- **Weaknesses:** it stays explanatory, and it will be hard to make the turning point *felt* rather than read. On a phone it risks looking like a beautiful infographic, not a film.

### C. «الأرض» — Terrain (topographic line world)
Wealth as a contour landscape drawn with hairlines. Saving builds a hill, and compounding makes the terrain rise and fold on its own, with a slow aerial camera move over the topography.
- **Strengths:** cinematic and unusual.
- **Weaknesses:** the metaphor is indirect, since hills don't intuitively "grow themselves". The 10% comparison is hard to show honestly, and dense line fields compress badly on social platforms.

### Choice: A, «الكتلة»
- It is the only direction where the core idea, moving from *you working for money* to *money working for you*, is a visible change in physics that people feel before they read it.
- The same object carries every scene, which gives natural match cuts: the dot becomes the cluster, the cluster becomes the sphere, the sphere becomes the comparison, and it ends back as the dot and then the full stop.
- The 10% comparison becomes physical and honest: each cobalt bead is worth 1,000 riyals, so the small mass releases 1 bead and the large mass releases 10.
- From B, it keeps the editorial typography and restraint.

## Shot list (30s)
| Time | Shot | Composition | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4.5 | Hook | Editorial type in the upper half, one dot in vast negative space | The question, and the first riyal |
| 4.5–10.5 | Effort | Extreme close-up, slowly rotating 3D cluster; grains carried in from below one by one | Every riyal is carried by you |
| 10.5–17 | Gravity | Pull back to a wide shot; the cluster fuses into a sphere; cobalt grains orbit and fall in faster and faster | Capital starts working |
| 17–24 | Proof | Editorial diagram: 10,000 vs 100,000 masses, with 10% released as beads (1 vs 10) | Same rate, bigger effect (illustrative) |
| 24–30 | Return | Pull back until the mass is a dot again; the dot becomes the full stop of «ابدأ اليوم.» | The million starts from the first riyal |

## Sound
Room tone plus a low drone that breathes; a dry tactile tick for each hand-carried grain; a soft granular rise as gravity takes over; one controlled sub impact at the reveal; near-silence before the end card; and a single clean tone when the dot lands as the full stop.
